MentiSystema is not a portfolio. It is a working methodology — built at the boundary where cognitive science crosses into adversarial territory and the tools stop being academic.

The foundation is Experimental Psychology and Cognitive Neuroscience. The behavioral architecture beneath Friction Bloom — stimulus, perturbation, adaptation, outcome — is clinical methodology reassigned to systems that were never meant to think.

Certified in Generative AI Engineering, Python, Web Development, AI and Machine Learning, and Social Engineering. Everything here is built from scratch and documented in motion.

Entered through AI and Python. The signal led to signals intelligence, penetration testing, hardware, adversarial psychology. Work that didn't fit an existing category built its own. That category is this.
